Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026 · Mutech ("we", "us") · Contact: muhamadgalal4@gmail.com

PulsePop is designed to need as little of your data as possible: no accounts, no logins, no email collection. This policy explains the minimal data the game and its service providers process.

1. Data we collect

  • Display name (you choose it, ≤16 chars, stored on-device and, if you post scores, shown publicly on leaderboards).
  • Random device ID (generated on first launch, stored on-device) — used as your anonymous leaderboard identity. No hardware identifiers.
  • Gameplay aggregates — scores, combo/difficulty stats, calibration offsets, ad interactions. Tied to the random device ID, never to your identity.
  • Ghost shares — score timelines you explicitly share via friend code. Shared ghosts auto-expire 7 days after upload.
  • Crash reports (Firebase Crashlytics) — anonymized stack traces and device-type info to fix bugs.
  • Ads data (Google AdMob) — Google may collect device/advertising identifiers to serve and measure ads per Google's Privacy Policy. Managed via the in-app consent form and your device ad settings.

2. Data we do NOT collect

No accounts, emails, contacts, precise location, microphone/camera input, or payment details (the game has no in-app purchases).

3. How data is used & retained

  • Leaderboards, ghost sharing, daily boards, and live tuning.
  • Ghost share payloads: deleted automatically 7 days after upload (plus delete-on-read garbage collection).
  • Aggregates are increment-only counters kept for balancing and live-ops.
  • Local data (scores, settings, calibration, dust) stays on your device; uninstalling deletes it.

4. Sharing & processors

We share data only with infrastructure processors acting on our behalf: Google Firebase (database/crash/analytics) and Google AdMob (ads). We do not sell personal data. Data may be stored/processed outside your country under these providers' safeguards.

5. Your rights & choices

  • Analytics opt-out: Settings → Analytics toggle (usage stats off; crash reports remain anonymized).
  • Ads consent: in-app consent form on first launch + device ad settings (reset/opt-out of ad ID).
  • Access/deletion: email muhamadgalal4@gmail.com from a request describing your display name/device ID; offensive board entries or ghosts can be removed. Uninstalling deletes all local data.

6. Children

PulsePop does not knowingly collect data from children under 13 beyond the anonymous gameplay data above, and contains third-party ads. Parents/guardians: use device parental controls and contact us for any removal request.

7. Changes

Material changes will be reflected here with a new "Last updated" date; continued play constitutes acceptance. For questions: muhamadgalal4@gmail.com.
